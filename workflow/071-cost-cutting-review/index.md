Show which categories are locked in and which have room to cut, so reviewing where to save money takes minutes instead of a manual walk through every category.

## User Stories

- As a user, I want to mark categories (or specific recurring items, e.g. sports subscriptions I keep) as "must spend" so that they drop out of my cost-cutting view.
- As a user, I want to see only the categories I can actually reduce (e.g. eating out, groceries) with what I spend on them, so that I know where the real opportunities are.
- As a user, I want to see how much a realistic cut (not to zero) in those categories would save per month and per year.

## Success

- A report lists flexible categories with current spend and potential savings.
- Fixed/must-spend categories and items are clearly separated from flexible ones.

### Out of Scope

- Multi-year projection of when fixed costs end (see the sibling feature on the yearly cost plan).

## Plan

To be shaped at ideation. Open questions: mark fixed at category level, item level, or both; how to express a target cut (percent, amount, budget).
